2. Monthly Wrapped Feature
2.1 Concept
Inspired by Spotify Wrapped, Monthly Wrapped is a personalized, visually stunning summary of each user's wellness journey over the past month. Designed to be highly shareable and celebratory, it transforms health data into an engaging story.
2.2 Timing & Availability
Release Schedule:

Generated on the 1st of each month at 12:00 AM SGT
Notification pushed: "Your [Month] Wrapped is ready! 🎁"
Available for viewing for 14 days
Can be accessed in "Past Wrappeds" archive indefinitely

2.6 Technical Specifications
2.6.1 Data Collection
Metrics Tracked:

App sessions (timestamp, duration)
All habit completions
Streak data
Steps (via HealthKit/Google Fit)
Event attendance
Social interactions
Achievement unlocks

2.6.2 Generation Process
Timeline:

T-2 days: Data aggregation begins
T-1 day: Comparisons calculated (percentiles, rankings)
T-0 (12:00 AM): Wrapped cards generated
T+5 minutes: Push notification sent
T+14 days: Moved to archive

2.6.3 Privacy Considerations
User Controls:

Option to hide specific stats from shared cards
Anonymous comparison data (no individual users named)
Can delete Wrapped from account entirely
School ranking can be hidden from shares

2.7 UI/UX Specifications
2.7.1 Navigation
Story Mode:

Tap right side: Next card
Tap left side: Previous card
Swipe up: View detailed breakdown
Swipe down: Exit to summary
Hold: Pause animation

Progress Indicator:

Top of screen: Story-style progress bars
Shows position in Wrapped journey

2.7.2 Animations
Card Transitions:

Fade + slide left for forward
Numbers count up when appearing
Charts animate in progressively
Mascot reacts to stats (happy for good, encouraging for low)

Celebration Moments:

Confetti for achievements
Fireworks for top percentile stats
Mascot dance for milestones

2.7.3 Sound Design (Optional)

Upbeat background music (toggleable)
Sound effects for stat reveals
Celebration sounds for achievements
All audio respects device silent mode

2.8 Engagement Hooks
Anticipation Building:

Week before: "Your January Wrapped is coming in 7 days!"
Day before: "Tomorrow you'll see how January went..."
Release: Push notification with preview stat

Social Pressure:

"23 of your friends have already viewed their Wrapped!"
"NP users are sharing their Wrapped - will you?"

Comparison Interest:

"How did your streak compare to other NP students?"
Preview of one comparison stat to drive click-through


3. Integration Between Features
3.1 Cross-Feature Synergies
Leaderboard → Wrapped:

Monthly Wrapped includes IHL ranking achievement
"You helped [School] reach #X this month!"
Personal contribution percentage highlighted

Wrapped → Leaderboard:

Sharing Wrapped earns points for IHL
Wrapped-driven referrals count toward school score
"Share your Wrapped to help NP climb the ranks!"

3.2 Notification Strategy
Leaderboard Notifications:

Daily: "NP is now #4! Help us reach #3 today!"
When passed: "Oh no! SP just passed NP. Rally your school!"
Weekly summary: "NP finished #4 this week. Here's what helped."

Wrapped Notifications:

Release: "Your January Wrapped is ready! 🎁"
Reminder (Day 7): "Haven't viewed your Wrapped yet? Your friends have!"
Expiring soon (Day 12): "Last chance! Your January Wrapped expires in 2 days"
