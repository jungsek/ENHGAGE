# ENHGAGE Writing Guide

## Introduction

Every word we write is part of the ENHGAGE story. This section covers the principles, voice, tone, and style that should guide all our communications. Whether you are writing in-app copy, marketing materials, or support documentation, these guidelines will help you produce content in the spirit of ENHGAGE and ensure consistency across all mediums.

**How:** We think learning should be nothing to fear and should feel all about progress. We have combined technology with the power of engagement to make learning a joy, not a chore. And because ENHGAGE is accessible, it really is open to everyone.

> **Our mission is to develop the most engaging learning experiences in the world and make them universally available.**

> **Everyone can ENHGAGE.**

## Voice

The ENHGAGE voice is how our brand personality comes through in words. Our voice has four key qualities that are felt in both what we say and how we say it. Working together, these qualities make everything we write sound uniquely ENHGAGE.

### Expressive

We use simple words and phrases to convey big feelings. We love learning. We love to help people grow. Expressive means we get excited and show it.

| Expressive Means | Not |
|------------------|-----|
| Brief | Long-winded |
| Active voice | Passive voice |
| Direct | Unclear |
| Excited | Indifferent |
| In your notifications | In your face |

### Playful

We bring creativity to the conversation. ENHGAGE brings a burst of creative energy to each user's day, like recess for the brain. Our voice is upbeat and ready for a challenge.

| Playful Means | Not |
|---------------|-----|
| Friendly | Touchy-feely |
| Jolly | Sarcastic or smug |
| Creative | Dry |
| Clever in quick doses | Intellectual |

### Embracing

Whoever you are, we are your biggest cheerleader. ENHGAGE is built on micro-successes. We make steps clear, celebrate users when they succeed, and encourage them when they stumble. We never alienate our users.

| Embracing Means | Not |
|-----------------|-----|
| Supportive | Aggressive |
| Gender-neutral (they, them) | Gendered (him, her) |
| Uses contractions | Writing words out |
| Clear | Complicated |
| Well-meaning | Mean-spirited |

### Worldly

We are interested and knowledgeable, and we have a broad worldview. ENHGAGE's reach is worldwide. We write content that speaks to everyone and excludes no one.

| Worldly Means | Not |
|---------------|-----|
| Interested | Smug |
| Knowledgeable | A know-it-all |
| Universal | Slang or references not everyone knows |
| Multilingual (but English first) | English only |

## Tone

Tone is all about reading the room. ENHGAGE's voice will always stay the same, but our tone changes depending on the situation.

### Content Considerations

Since learning is such a huge concept that everyone can relate to, ENHGAGE content runs the gamut. Sometimes we will share funny pop-culture references. Sometimes we will share content that is much more serious. This content can coexist if we are careful about tone.

For some people, learning means escaping lives they do not want. When telling these stories, write with respect. Tone down the exuberance (and exclamation points). Treat this content not like content, but like someone's life.

### Context Considerations

When users succeed, we **celebrate** them. Bust out the exclamation points!

| Like This | Not This |
|-----------|----------|
| Correct! | You are correct |
| Awesome work! | You have successfully passed this level |

When users stumble, we **support** them. This is the time for friendly, helpful language—but do not overdo it. We want them to keep going!

| Like This | Not This |
|-----------|----------|
| Not quite correct. Try again! | Incorrect. |
| Oh no! You're out of tries. Want to review the lesson? | We're very sorry, but you don't have any tries left. |

## Style

These style guidelines help us construct our content to be clear and consistent.

### Numerals

Write all numbers numerically, even numbers under 10. But if a number starts a sentence, spell it out. If the number is over 999, include a comma. But do not include a comma for XP or other currency totals in the product.

### Punctuation

Do not punctuate headlines, except with an exclamation point. Do not punctuate subheads, unless with an exclamation point or if it is a multi-sentence subhead. Never use punctuation in a button. Use the serial comma (Oxford comma). Avoid semicolons—use an em dash or break long sentences into two simple sentences.

### Capitalization

Put button copy in all caps. Use sentence case for headers, subheads, blog post titles, and page titles. Use title case for video titles and titles of stories.

### Contractions

Use contractions. They make our writing feel more natural and conversational.

### Active Voice

Use active voice whenever possible. It is more direct and engaging than passive voice.
