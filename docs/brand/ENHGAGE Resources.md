## Typography

### Brand Fonts

Our brand fonts are available for use in all design applications. Please contact the brand team to obtain the font files.

| Typeface | Usage | Weights |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Primary Typeface | Headlines, CTAs | Bold |
| Secondary Typeface | Body copy, subheads | Regular, Light |

### Substitute Font

In situations where our brand fonts are not available, please use the free Google Font **Nunito**.

- **Google Fonts Link:** [fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito)
- **Weights to Use:** Light (300), Regular (400), Bold (700)
