# ENHGAGE Brand Identity

---

## Introduction

This document establishes the core visual identity for ENHGAGE. How we write our name, how we use our symbols—these are the closest we get to a "signature" for ENHGAGE. How they appear, whether tiny on a screen or enormous on a billboard, becomes a crucial way for our users and partners to find and trust us.

---

## Logotype

Our typographic signature uses custom letterforms that embody the spirit of ENHGAGE: modern, approachable, and dynamic. The letterforms feature subtle curves that convey warmth and accessibility while maintaining a professional, tech-forward aesthetic.

The logotype can be used in one of three colors: **Deep Teal**, **White**, or **Cool Gray**.

### Clear Space

To allow our logotype to stand out, do not place any objects such as graphics or text in the clear space shown. The minimum clear space is equal to the x-height of the lowercase letters in the ENHGAGE logotype on all sides.

### Minimum Sizes

The logotype should always be legible. Never use it at sizes smaller than specified.

| Application | Minimum Size |
| --- | --- |
| Screen | 100px wide |
| Print | 35mm / 1.375in wide |

### General Positioning

When creating a composition, place the logo in one of the four corners, or center it on the central vertical axis. Take advantage of the space and make the logo as large as possible. Be bold, be proud, make it big. Avoid using the logotype at sizes smaller than one-third of the canvas width.

### Rotation

Typically, we place our logotype flat on a 0° angle. Horizontal is our default rotation state. This applies to use in our app, on our website, and most advertising formats. Vertical rotation (90°) may be used sparingly for unusual formats like vertical banners or flags, but this should be the exception rather than the rule.

### Logotype Don'ts

To maintain the integrity of our brand, please observe the following restrictions:

| Don't | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Change the color of the logo | Maintains brand recognition |
| Squash or stretch the logo | Preserves proportions |
| Recreate with a different font | Ensures consistency |
| Outline the logo | Maintains visual integrity |
| Fill the logo with images | Keeps focus on the wordmark |
| Place over busy backgrounds | Ensures legibility |
| Skew the logo | Only 0° rotation is accepted |
| Fill with gradients | Maintains flat design aesthetic |
| Change spacing or proportions | Preserves design intent |

## Icon

Because ENHGAGE's presence largely exists online, the consistent appearance of our icon is critical in ensuring our audience recognizes our brand across various spaces. In many cases, this symbol is the first introduction that people have to ENHGAGE.

### Avatars

Our icon is our face on social accounts, keeping our brand personality front and center. It can be safely used at a wide variety of sizes, as small as 16px like our favicon. In instances where we know the avatar will be displayed as a square (with or without rounded corners), the icon should touch the left and right edges of the frame. When the masking shape approaches a circle, there should be space between the icon and the edge on either side.

## Typography

We're an engagement company, so the words we write and the typefaces we choose matter. Our typography is clean, legible, and modern, designed to create a clear and consistent visual hierarchy across all touchpoints.

### Primary Typeface: Nunito Font

Our typeface is bold, confident, and attention-grabbing. It is used for large, brief headlines, left-aligned lists, and key messaging.

**Usage Guidelines:**

- Set leading between 100–110%

- Set tracking to -20, kerning to 0 optical

- Never use below 30pt in print or 30px onscreen

- Avoid long sentences—if text is longer than 10 words, use the secondary typeface

- Always use sentence case, never all caps (except for buttons)

- Preferred alignment is left-aligned; never use fully justified

- Text should never be hyphenated

### Secondary Typeface: Body Font

Our secondary typeface is used for longer sections of text, subheadings, and all body copy. It is highly legible at smaller sizes and has a friendly, approachable feel.

**Usage Guidelines:**

- Set leading to 140%

- Set tracking to 0, kerning to 0 optical

- Never use below 14pt in print or 14px onscreen

- Type is usually left-aligned or centered; never use fully justified

- Text should never be hyphenated

### Hierarchy

To achieve a harmonious relationship between our logotype and headlines, use the secondary typeface to ensure there is a contrast in weight and scale. The secondary typeface should be used in a light weight when paired with our logotype. When using the secondary typeface with the logo, the ideal scale is 1.5 times the x-height of the logotype.



### Typography Don'ts

| Don't | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Combine typefaces in the same sentence | Maintains clarity |
| Use headline font in multiple secondary colors | Preserves hierarchy |
| Set headline font in neutral colors (except one-color printing) | Ensures visibility |
| Have type running off the display area | Maintains professionalism |
| Set headline font in Title Case | Maintains brand style |
| Set body headline at the same size as the logo | Preserves hierarchy |

## Imagery

ENHGAGE is a highly visual brand. Illustration and photography are key parts of our identity, and we use them to create a warm, inviting, and engaging experience for our users.

### Illustration

Illustration is a core visual style for ENHGAGE. It feels like us, and our characters and icons are a critical part of the ENHGAGE brand story. Illustrations should be simple, clean, and use a limited color palette drawn from our brand colors.

### Photography

The photos we use reflect the kind of company we are and the company we would like to keep. We showcase authentic, real, interesting people with real-life stories to tell. All our photography is led by people who use ENHGAGE, revealing their stories and personalities. A sense of place should be evident in environments—the commute to work, learning at home, and activities with friends. When possible, we use images with an element of teal in clothing or surroundings.

## Brand Family

As we grow, introducing more features and products, our family grows with us. This section provides guidance on how to create and use sub-brands within the ENHGAGE ecosystem.

### Sub-Brands

For sub-brands and initiatives that do not have their own rules, a generic layout exists to help keep our brand family consistent. The core wordmark and sub-brand title share an x-height and are separated by a space equal in width to the counter of the "E" character in "ENHGAGE." Since the two elements are essentially the same size, we rely on weight to define hierarchy.

### Premium Tier

Our premium service has its own specific logotype. The colorways of the premium brand change to show our users that they are experiencing an enhanced version of the app. In the interest of legibility and consistency, ensure the placement and relationship of logo elements remains intact.
