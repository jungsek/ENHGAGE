# ENHGAGE Marketing Guide

---

## Introduction

When creating ENHGAGE assets for marketing materials, we have just a few guidelines we would like you to follow. These are the greatest hits of ENHGAGE's visual brand. This guide will help you create materials using all the critical elements of the ENHGAGE visual identity.

---

## Assets

### Colors in Marketing

Our marketing materials should primarily use our core brand colors. **Deep Teal** should be the dominant color, creating instant brand recognition. Our secondary colors should be used strategically to add visual interest and create hierarchy.

**Background Guidelines:**

| Background Type | Usage |
|-----------------|-------|
| Deep Teal | Primary marketing backgrounds, hero sections |
| Light Teal | Secondary backgrounds, callout sections |
| White/Snow | Body content, clean layouts |
| Secondary Colors | Accent elements, CTAs, highlights |

### Mascot/Character Usage

Our mascot (if applicable) is the most recognizable element of our brand. People love it. That is why it often gets a starring role in our marketing and advertising materials. Use it to express excitement for ENHGAGE, convey a range of emotions, or commemorate a special occasion.

**On Backgrounds:** We only use our mascot on select secondary background colors because its vibrant colors can easily vibrate and look harsh against other colors. Test your compositions to ensure visual harmony.

### Cast of Characters

Our characters are the stars of our app. They are quickly becoming ENHGAGE ambassadors in marketing and advertising, demonstrating in their diversity that everyone can ENHGAGE. Use them all at once, in pairs, or one at a time.

Posing a character adds personality and quirk to their design and ultimately tells more of their story. Try not to use characters in a static, expressionless state, which could make them feel lifeless.

**On Backgrounds:** Our characters can be used on a broader range of core and secondary colors, because they tend to feature more hues and do not cause too much vibration.

## Social Media Guidelines

### Platform Presence

Our social media presence should be engaging, interactive, and consistent with our brand identity. We should use a variety of content formats, including:

- Educational content and tips
- User success stories
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Interactive polls and questions
- Celebratory posts for user milestones

### Tone on Social

We can be looser with the rules on social media. Use emoji that match the message, whichever and however many make sense. But remember: a little emoji go a long way. Emoji are super useful—they convey information and grab attention. But do not overdo it.

### Hashtags

Use branded hashtags consistently:
- #ENHGAGE
- #EveryoneCanENHGAGE
- #LearningMadeEngaging
