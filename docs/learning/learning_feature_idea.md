Solution Plan: ENHGAGE Learning Format
The Core Challenge
We need one consistent format that can transform 20 diverse programmes (from K-pop fitness to advance care planning) into engaging micro-learning—while avoiding the pitfalls of AI-generated content that feels inauthentic.

Proposed Format: "Lesson Cards"
Think of it as Instagram Stories meets Duolingo—a swipeable, tap-through card system with consistent structure but flexible content types.
Why This Format Works
Consideration
How Lesson Cards Address It
Consistency
Same structure across all programmes = predictable UX
Authenticity
Prioritizes real NHG photos, expert quotes, actual programme materials over AI generation
Youth-friendly
Vertical, swipeable, bite-sized—native to how youth consume content
Adaptable
Can hold different content types (text, images, quizzes, scenarios)
Low friction
2-3 minutes per lesson, no videos to buffer/load
Real-world connection
Every lesson ends with a CTA to actual NHG programmes

Preference-Driven Delivery
During onboarding, users select their preferred pillars. This determines:
Home feed content - Lesson cards surfaced from selected pillars
Streak contributions - Completing lessons from preferred pillars counts toward daily habits
Notifications - "New lesson in Nutrition" only if they selected that pillar
Recommendations - "Based on your interest in Fitness, try this new KpopX lesson"
Preference updates: Users can modify pillar preferences anytime in Settings → My Interests
